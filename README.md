This project's main goal is to put a basic Inventory Management System in the Department of Health to care for Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). The inventory of materials for PPE that will be transferred from suppliers to hospitals is to be managed by the system. The procedure of this system is aimed at creating the Inventory using Python. It involves the tasks of registration, item creation, sanitation of inventories, tracking of inventory, searching elements, and production of reports. The main purpose is to track and manage PPE products better, which are very important for normal supply conditions and hospital functionality.
